KMU Unified Draft Policy — Quick Reference

Document Identification

Title: KMU Unified Statutory Policy on Institutional Organogram, Job Descriptions, Workload Allocation and Performance Management
Policy reference: KMU/REG/POL/2026/01-REV
Status: Pending re-vetting and re-submission to the Syndicate; not yet operative.
Portal cycle configuration: 01 January to 31 December (calendar year); align this correction in the final approved policy/notification.
Intended approval route: The competent statutory bodies identified in the draft, subject to the applicable law and formal notification.

Objective vs Subjective Structure

  • Objective Domains (A, B, C, E): Quantifiable, verified teaching load (excluding outside/visiting teaching), research grants ledger (funding agency & volume in PKR), publications, supervision, and institutional charges.
  • Appraising Officer Domain (Section D): Proposed qualitative evaluation by the designated Appraising Officer across 13 professional criteria and 5 CPD goals.
  • Research pilot calibration: Draft output points/caps are retained, but the portal uses proposed profile-specific annual full-credit benchmarks of 15/20/30/45/60 raw points. The original theoretical cap is 200. The benchmarks require KMU data validation and ORIC/ASRB/statutory approval.
  • 80% display benchmark: Green means a dimension reached at least 80% of its available weighted-point ceiling; red means it is below 80%. This visual aid does not replace the overall rating bands.
  • Open items: Research normalization, grant sub-rules, service rubric, peer/CPD rubric and clinical/patient-care rubric remain proposed/configurable pending ratification.
  • Precedence: Current law, statutes, appointment orders and valid notifications prevail over this calculator and over any unnotified future-state organogram.

Automatic Profile Assignment

  • Dedicated research cadres/postdocs use the Research Cadres / Postdocs profile.
  • Senior Registrars, Medical Officers, Consultants and KMU-H&RC assignments use the Clinical profile.
  • Regional KMU Institutes of Health Sciences use the Teaching-Focused profile.
  • Other faculty default to Balanced unless a documented annual assignment supports another profile.
  • Academic rank and TTS status alone do not change the weights. Leadership appointments change the draft reporting line, not the substantive profile.
