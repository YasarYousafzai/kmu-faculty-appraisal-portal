Step 3: Section B — Research, Grants, Supervision & Commercialization

Score selected, independently reviewed research outputs and verified annual research work. The old draft point schedule remains below for audit trace only.

Weight: 30%
Proposed quality-first pilot, not KMU-approved: Section B provisionally scores up to three distinct research outputs (60 points), annual research execution (25 points), and research mentorship or translation (15 points). A grant is optional evidence, never a required annual win. A fourth paper or further quantity does not increase this score. The faculty member enters a self-assessment first; the Appraising Officer must review the evidence and quality ratings before a final draft dossier is complete. The old policy draft instead divides capped raw points by 200; its historical schedule below is retained for comparison, not for the weighted score. ORIC, ASRB and the competent approval forum must review this departure before personnel use.
How to choose provisional research ratings

Use these proposed anchors consistently across comparable faculty. They are guidance for the pilot, not approved KMU standards; the reviewer must inspect the work and record any correction.

  • Each selected output (20): rigor and ethics 0–8; significance 0–6; documented personal contribution 0–4; relevance or usable dissemination 0–2. A journal metric or author position alone never proves quality. Retracted, unverifiable or duplicate work should not receive credit.
  • Research execution (25): 0 for no evidenced progress; 1–9 limited; 10–17 documented partial progress; 18–22 substantial progress; 23–25 completed high-quality milestones against an approved plan. A documented competitive proposal or award can add up to 5 within this same 25-point ceiling, not above it.
  • Mentorship or translation (15): 0 for none; 1–5 limited documented work; 6–10 meaningful trainee, method, data or translation progress; 11–15 independently verified completion or uptake. Do not count teaching or patient care again.

B1 Pilot: Up to Three Selected Research Outputs Maximum 60 pts

Choose at most three of your strongest distinct outputs for this calendar year. A DOI or stable institutional record ID is required for each scored item. Enter a provisional self-rating for scientific rigor and ethics (0–8), significance (0–6), your documented contribution (0–4), and relevance or usable dissemination (0–2). The Appraising Officer must check these ratings against the work itself. Journal impact factor is not a substitute for judging the work.

Objective vs Subjective Structure

  • Faculty evidence domains (A, B, C, E): Teaching credits and QEC, selected research outputs with provisional quality self-ratings, documented research progress, institutional service, and clinical service where applicable. Section B needs independent reviewer verification; the draft-policy publication/grant count schedule remains visible only as an audit trace.

  • Research pilot calibration: The weighted Section B score uses up to three independently reviewed outputs (60 points), annual execution (25 points), and research mentorship or translation (15 points). ORIC/ASRB and the competent forum must approve any operational use.
  • 80% display benchmark: Green means a dimension reached at least 80% of its available weighted-point ceiling; red means it is below 80%. This visual aid does not replace the overall rating bands.
  • Open items: The quality-first research rubric, grant sub-rules, service rubric, peer/CPD rubric and clinical/patient-care rubric remain proposed/configurable pending ratification. Comparable-faculty percentile categories are deferred until KMU has suitable cohort data.
  • Precedence: Current law, statutes, appointment orders and valid notifications prevail over this calculator and over any unnotified future-state organogram.

Automatic Profile Assignment

  • Dedicated research cadres/postdocs use the Research Cadres / Postdocs profile.
  • Senior Registrars, Medical Officers, Consultants and KMU-H&RC assignments use the Clinical profile.
  • Regional KMU Institutes of Health Sciences use the Teaching-Focused profile.
  • Other faculty default to Balanced unless a documented annual assignment supports another profile.
  • Academic rank and TTS status alone do not change the weights. Leadership appointments change the draft reporting line, not the substantive profile.
