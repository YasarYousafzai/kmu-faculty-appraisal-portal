Proposed pilot teaching calculation: each faculty-entered course review contributes in proportion to the contact hours taught. Enter theory and practical credits separately: 1 theory credit = 16 contact hours; 1 practical credit = 32 contact hours, so a 1+1 course represents 48 contact hours and 2 taught credits. The resulting student-review mean is multiplied by a capped workload factor based on the provisional rank target below. Course entries calculate immediately without a separate KMU verification step. This rubric is not KMU-approved for personnel use.
HEC credit-hour and designation workload guidance (context only)

The portal uses the requested 16-week, 1-theory-credit = 16 and 1-practical-credit = 32 contact-period convention. The HEC Pakistan Qualification Register explanation describes one theory period and two practical/lab hours weekly per credit; another HEC uniform-semester guideline describes three practical hours. Confirm the applicable KMU programme scheme before adopting the conversion formally.

This pilot uses an HEC mathematics curriculum recommendation as an illustrative, unapproved KMU teaching-volume target: Professor 3, Associate Professor 6, Assistant Professor 9, and Lecturer 12 taught credits per semester. Meritorious Professor is provisionally mapped to the Professor target; the HEC example does not name that designation. For the January–December pilot, two regular semesters are assumed, giving annual targets of 6, 12, 18, and 24 credits. The workload factor is credits taught ÷ annual target, capped at 100%; an approved exemption declaration waives this proration. Other designations have no invented automatic target. These figures are discipline-specific, not a universal KMU medical-faculty rule: the business-school accreditation standard uses different maxima. KMU must approve or replace these pilot targets before formal use.

Objective vs Subjective Structure

  • Faculty evidence domains: A teaching (contact-hour-weighted mean of faculty-entered course reviews); B research; C patient care when applicable; D institutional service. Theory and practical credits determine each course's contribution.
  • Appraisal Committee assessment: Proposed qualitative evaluation by the designated Appraising Officer or committee across 13 professional criteria and 5 CPD goals.
  • Research pilot calibration: the original draft B1–B5 count tables and scoring have been restored: publications (50), books/chapters (20), grants/proposals (50), supervision (40), innovation (40), divided by 200. The old W/X/Y-to-impact-factor equivalence is not repeated because it is not established. The resulting research percentage will be low for ordinary annual output; KMU must validate this before personnel use.
  • 80% display benchmark: Green means a dimension reached at least 80% of its available weighted-point ceiling; red means it is below 80%. This visual aid does not replace the overall rating bands.
  • Open items: The paper impact-factor bands, grant sub-rules, contact-hour-weighted teaching rule, service rubric, committee/CPD rubric and clinical/patient-care rubric remain proposed pending ratification. Comparable-faculty percentile categories are deferred until KMU has suitable cohort data.
  • Precedence: Current law, statutes, appointment orders and valid notifications prevail over this calculator and over any unnotified future-state organogram.

Automatic Profile Assignment

  • Dedicated research cadres/postdocs use the Research Cadres / Postdocs profile.
  • Senior Registrars, Medical Officers, Consultants and KMU-H&RC assignments use the Clinical profile.
  • Regional KMU Institutes of Health Sciences use the Teaching-Focused profile.
  • Other faculty default to Balanced unless a documented annual assignment supports another profile.
  • Academic rank and TTS status alone do not change the weights. Leadership appointments change the draft reporting line, not the substantive profile.
