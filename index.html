Appraisal Formula Overview (Section 9.2)

S_final = MAX(0, S_Teaching + S_Research + S_Service + S_Peer + S_Clinical - Penalty_RedFlag)

  • Teaching (S_A): QEC score normalized to 100, prorated against minimum Workload Units if shortfall occurs without approved leave.
  • Research (S_B): Capped at 200 raw points across 5 subcategories: B1 Publications (max 50), B2 Books (max 20), B3 Grants (max 50, applied capped at 20), B4 Supervision (max 40), and B5 Innovation/Patents (max 40).
  • Service (S_C): Capped at 100 raw points across committee memberships, convenorships, policy authorship, and facility charges.
  • Peer & CPD (S_D): 13 Core Parameters (1–6 scale, max 78) + 5 CPD Targets (0–2 scale, max 10) = 88-point denominator.
  • Clinical (S_E): Max 100 raw points across unit administration, patient workload, on-call compliance, and bedside tutorials.

Performance Ratings & Due Process

  • ≥ 85.0%: Outstanding (Exceeds Expectations with Distinction)
  • 75.0% – 84.9%: Very Good (Exceeds Expectations)
  • 60.0% – 74.9%: Good (Meets Expectations)
  • 50.0% – 59.9%: Average (Needs Improvement — Discretionary PIP)
  • < 50.0%: Unsatisfactory (MANDATORY PIP for 3–6 months under Sec. 9.5)
  • Appellate Rights: 07 calendar days from formal notification to lodge written appeal to the Appellate Committee (Sec. 9.6).
